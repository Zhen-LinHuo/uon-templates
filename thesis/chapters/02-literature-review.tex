\chapter{Literature Review}
\label{ch:literature}

Organise the review around the \glspl{rq}, not around a chronological list
of papers. For each strand, state what is established, what is contested,
and what is missing.

\section{Strand one}

Summary of the strand, its principal results, and its limitations.

\section{Strand two}

Summary of the second strand. Give author-year citations, for example
\autocite{helbing2000}, or parenthetical ones \parencite{bonabeau2002}.

\section{The gap}

State the gap the thesis fills, in one paragraph, and connect it to
\rqone{} and \rqtwo{}.

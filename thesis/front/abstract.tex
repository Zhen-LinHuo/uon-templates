\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}

Write the abstract here. A thesis abstract is normally one page: the
problem, the approach, the principal results, and why they matter.

Avoid citations in the abstract and keep it within the word limit the
University applies to the thesis as a whole (100,000 words for a PhD,
60,000 for an MPhil, inclusive of appendices, footnotes, tables and
bibliography).
